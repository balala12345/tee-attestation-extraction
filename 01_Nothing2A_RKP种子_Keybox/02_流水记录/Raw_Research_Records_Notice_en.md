# Raw research record notice

This directory contains copied and sanitized source records. Commands, return codes, failure branches, hashes, timestamps, and decision notes are preserved; the source files were not moved or deleted.

Host absolute paths, user names, device serials, tokens, PEM/private-key blocks, and raw 32-byte materials were removed or replaced. Device-side paths and patch protocol fields remain where they are needed to understand the operation. The curated tutorial, ledger, and principles documents have Chinese and English versions; the raw evidence records retain their original language and are indexed here.
