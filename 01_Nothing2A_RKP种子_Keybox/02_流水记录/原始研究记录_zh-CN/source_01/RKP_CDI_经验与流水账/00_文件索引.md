## 脚本与客户端

- `scripts/rkp_research/patch_0706_rkp_sideleak.py`：最终 direct sideleak patch 脚本。
- `scripts/rkp_research/run_direct_csr_batch.py`：生成 12 个 production `MacedPublicKey` 并侧漏 scalar 的 direct 批处理 runner。
- `scripts/rkp_research/rkp_tee_probe_csr.c` / `rkp_tee_probe_csr`：direct keygen/CSR native client。
- `scripts/rkp_research/rkp_binder_csr_client.cpp` / `rkp_binder_csr_client`：直接调用 stock HAL Binder `generateCertificateRequest` 的 native client。
- `scripts/rkp_research/run_binder_csr_from_direct.py`：把 direct run 中的 12 个 `MacedPublicKey` 推给 stock Binder CSR。
- `scripts/rkp_research/fetch_google_geek.py`：获取 fresh GEEK/challenge。
- `scripts/rkp_research/build_and_send_signcerts.py`：CBOR、COSE、P-256、Google endpoint 相关基础函数。
- `scripts/rkp_research/build_and_send_signcerts_batch.py`：用 Binder CSR 输出和 12 个 private scalar 构造并发送 batch `signCertificates`。
- `scripts/rkp_research/verify_rkp_sideleak.py`：P-256 scalar/public 验证与调试 XML/PEM 生成。
- `scripts/rkp_research/run_stock_hal_mem_capture.py`：捕获 stock 成功 CSR 的真实 TCI 布局。
- `scripts/rkp_research/run_stock_rkpd_restore.py`：恢复 stock TA/HAL/RKPD 状态。
- `scripts/rkp_research/patch_0706_rkp_obj216.py`、`patch_0706_rkp_recopy_stock.py`、`patch_0706_rkp_noop_branch.py`、`patch_0706_rkp_sideleak_biglen.py`：失败路线和控制实验 patch。

## 失败与控制实验

- `failed_and_control_runs/signcerts_outer_variants_failed/`：外层 `signCertificates` 变体全部失败，说明不能靠改外层 CBOR 绕过。
- `failed_and_control_runs/direct_one_key_prod_failed/`：早期 one-key direct production CSR 失败。
- `failed_and_control_runs/direct_4e_sideleak_failed_reference/`：`4e` sideleak 路线失败参考。
- `failed_and_control_runs/system_noop_loaded_ta_failed/`：no-op branch patch 只要系统实际加载 modified `4d` TA 就失败。
- `failed_and_control_runs/system_recopy_loaded_ta_failed/`：recopy stock patch 系统加载后失败。
- `failed_and_control_runs/system_sideleak_stocklen_memscan_failed/`：system sideleak + memscan 失败。
- `failed_and_control_runs/system_obj216_capture_failed/`：obj216 方案系统路径失败。
